Questions to ask before work begins

A careful hiring conversation should leave you clear about the proposed work. Ask for the contractor’s license information, confirm that it applies to the work being discussed, and check the information with the appropriate California state source. You can also ask who will perform the work and what happens if the initial assessment points to a different issue.

Use this short checklist before agreeing to a job:

  • What work do you recommend, and what problem is it meant to address?
  • What is included in the visit or proposed work?
  • Are there additional steps you’ll discuss with me before proceeding?
  • Can you provide your license information so I can verify it?
  • What access to the system or property should I arrange?

Avoid choosing a contractor based only on a quick diagnosis over the phone. Be wary of unclear explanations, pressure to approve work you don’t understand, or a refusal to provide information you’ve reasonably asked for. Ask for the recommendation in plain language before you decide.
